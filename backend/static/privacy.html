ManaLife Calendar

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 February 2026  •  Last updated: 7 February 2026

Short version: ManaLife Calendar is an offline Telugu Panchang & devotional calendar app. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your preferences (theme, language, city, notification toggle) stay on your device.

1. Who we are

ManaLife Calendar (the “App”) is a mobile application that provides Telugu Panchang information (Tithi, Nakshatram, Vara, Yoga, Karana), Rahukalam / Yamagandam / Gulika Kalam timings, sunrise & sunset times, Hindu festival & Vratham details, daily Slokas, and devotional quotes. In this policy “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the publisher of ManaLife Calendar.

2. Information we do NOT collect

The App is designed to be fully offline-first. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account information (the App has no login).
  • Your precise device location or GPS coordinates.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or any other tracking identifiers.
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry sent to us.

3. Information stored locally on your device

The following preferences are saved on your device using local encrypted storage. They never leave your device:

  • Your selected language (English or Telugu).
  • Your selected theme (Light or Dark).
  • Your selected city (chosen from a pre-defined list — used only to compute local sunrise, sunset, Rahukalam, Yamagandam and Gulika Kalam).
  • Whether you have enabled the daily notification reminder.

You can clear all of this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing app storage from your device settings.

4. Notifications

If you enable Daily Notifications in Settings, the App will schedule a local reminder at approximately 6:30 AM each morning showing today's Panchang. These notifications are generated entirely on your device by the operating system. We do not use push notifications, we do not operate any notification server, and no notification content is sent to or from our servers.

5. Third-party services

Hero images shown on the Home, Festivals and Devotion screens are loaded from public content delivery networks (Unsplash and Pexels). These providers may log basic connection information such as IP address for the sole purpose of serving the image, as any web request does. We do not share any personal information with these providers.

The App does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social-login SDKs.

6. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

7. Security

Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk. Your on-device preferences are stored using the platform's standard secure storage APIs.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA and similar regulations you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Because we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or export on your behalf. You may exercise the equivalent right at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the App from your device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at support@manalife-calendar.app.
